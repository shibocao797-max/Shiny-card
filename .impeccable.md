## Design Context

### Users
Players and creators building a personal collection of named cards. They need to create, find, revisit, and add notes to each card's dedicated space.

### Brand Personality
Luminous, curious, tactile. The experience should feel like tending a personal archive with a little sense of discovery.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use the references as interaction cues: a collectible, dimensional card and particles that disperse and re-form around the selected card. Keep the interface dark and atmospheric, balanced with warm paper-like text, citrus highlights, and restrained coral details. Avoid generic cyberpunk neon, decorative glow blobs, and motion that competes with reading.

### Design Principles
1. Particle motion communicates selection and transformation.
2. The archive remains readable and useful even when motion is reduced or unavailable.
3. Creation, search, and opening a card are direct and reversible.
4. Card data belongs to the player and persists locally by default.
